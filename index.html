Nokutenda's Airline Passenger Analysis

Graphic 1: International Passenger Growth (1949-1960)

What this represents: This line chart tracks the monthly number of international airline passengers over an 11-year period.

Why it's important: It visualizes the steady, exponential growth of the aviation industry. It shows that despite small fluctuations, the overall trend of global travel was rising rapidly during the mid-20th century.

Graphic 2: Monthly Distribution & Seasonality

What this represents: This box-and-whisker plot groups the passengers by month to show the distribution and variance for each month across the entire dataset.

Why it's interesting: It highlights a predictable "summer peak" in July and August. This seasonality is crucial for airlines to understand when to increase staff and flight frequency to meet higher demand.
