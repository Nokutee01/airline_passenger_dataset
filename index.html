Aviation Growth & Seasonality Analysis (1949-1960)

The Post-War Growth Trend

What: A continuous upward trend in monthly passenger counts.
Why: Driven by the "Jet Age" technological shift and a booming post-war middle class that began viewing air travel as a standard lifestyle choice rather than a luxury.

Seasonal Demand Spikes

What: Consistent peaks in July and August with lows in November/February.
Why: Reflects the establishment of the "Summer Vacation" tradition and school holiday schedules, which forces airlines to maximize fleet availability during these months.

Dataset Source: International Airline Passengers (CSV)
